## Objective

Record `finding` decisions before revising a `spec`, and require renewed approval before implementation resumes.

See the
[specification](https://github.com/emilioSp/pi-maestro/blob/839ab9eb9753df22804d750657a616a9959c2c47/.specs/20261015-154812-route-finding-driven-spec-revisions-through-drafting-spec/spec.md).

## Completed When

- All current decisions are saved before entering `drafting-spec`; revision takes priority in mixed batches.
- The `owner` reviews the same revised `spec` and gives renewed `GREEN FLAG` before separate approval and another `builder` `run`.
- Direct approval from `finding-decisions` is rejected.
